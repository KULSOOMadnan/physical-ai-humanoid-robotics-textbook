# Module 1: The Robot Operating System 2 (ROS 2).

Welcome to Module 1 of the **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Textbook**.

If a robot were a living organism, **ROS 2 would be its nervous system**—the invisible infrastructure that allows sensors to perceive the world, algorithms to think, and actuators to move with purpose. In this module, you will dive into **ROS 2**, the industry-standard framework that enables modern robots to communicate, coordinate, and operate in real-world environments.

This module lays the **technical foundation** for everything that follows in this course. Whether you are building autonomous mobile robots, humanoids, or intelligent agents that interact with the physical world, understanding ROS 2 is non-negotiable.

---

## Why This Module Matters

Humanoid and physical AI systems are fundamentally different from traditional software systems. They must operate in **real time**, handle **uncertain sensor data**, and coordinate multiple subsystems simultaneously. ROS 2 exists to solve exactly these problems.

In this module, you will learn not just *what* ROS 2 is, but *why* it is designed the way it is—and how it enables scalable, modular, and reliable robotic systems.

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

- Understand the principles of **Physical AI** and **embodied intelligence**
- Explain why **humanoid robots** are uniquely suited for human-centered environments
- Identify and understand common **robotic sensor systems**, including:
  - Cameras and depth sensors  
  - LIDAR  
  - IMUs  
  - Force and torque sensors
- Understand the **core architecture of ROS 2**, including:
  - Nodes  
  - Topics  
  - Services  
  - Actions
- Set up a complete **ROS 2 development environment**
- Create and manage **ROS 2 workspaces and packages**
- Implement **message passing and communication patterns**
- Use **launch files and ROS 2 tools** to manage complex robotic systems
- Build basic ROS 2 applications for **robot control and sensor data processing**
