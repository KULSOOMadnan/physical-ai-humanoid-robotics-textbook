# Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)

Welcome to Module 2! In this module, you will dive into simulation environments that serve as digital twins for your robots. You'll master Gazebo and Unity for creating realistic virtual environments, testing algorithms safely, and developing sim-to-real transfer techniques.

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this module, you will:

- Understand the critical role of simulation in robotics development
- Learn about different simulation platforms including Gazebo, Unity, and NVIDIA Isaac Sim
- Master the concept of digital twins and their components
- Explore setting up simulation environments for your specific humanoid robot projects
- Understand kinematics and dynamics in the context of humanoid robots
- Learn about balance and locomotion in simulation environments
- Master manipulation and grasping techniques in simulation
- Understand how to create high-fidelity physics models
- Learn about sensor simulation and perception in virtual environments
- Implement sim-to-real transfer strategies

## Module Structure

This module is organized into several chapters that progressively build your understanding of simulation environments:

- **Chapter 4**: Introduction to Simulation Environments (Gazebo & Unity)
- **Chapter 5**: Setting up Simulation Environments
- **Chapter 6**: Digital Twin Concepts
- **Chapter 7**: Kinematics and Dynamics for Humanoids
- **Chapter 8**: Balance and Locomotion for Humanoids
- **Chapter 9**: Manipulation & Grasping in Humanoids
